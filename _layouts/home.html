A good run, your way

Make Tuesday your running night

01

Meet by the harbour

We meet Tuesdays from 6:00pm at the Cockle Bay Wharf Bird Fountain. Runs leave at 6:15pm.

02

Choose your distance

Short courses are around 6–8km and long courses around 11–13km. The routes change, so check the weekly schedule.

03

Run at your pace

Pace groups range from 4:30 to 6:15 min/km. There’s bag drop, plus post-run meals and drink specials.

New to the group?

Just show up and run.
